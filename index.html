About Me

I am a computer science researcher working across spatial intelligence, multimodal perception, wireless human sensing, and embodied AI. I am currently a Research Intern with the LycheeAI / Litchi Lab Humanoid Group under the supervision of Prof. Yiming Li.

My work begins with a simple question: how can an intelligent system maintain a coherent model of a changing environment when its observations are incomplete? I approach this through long-form visual reasoning, WiFi and mmWave sensing, and representations that connect perception to embodied action.

My long-term goal is to develop general-purpose humanoid robots that can recognise human needs and support healthcare and nursing work safely, naturally, and reliably.

Research Direction

What I study, why it matters, and how I intend to develop it.

01 · What

Persistent spatial understanding

I investigate how models can preserve identity, geometry, and temporal state across long sensory streams, rather than interpreting each observation in isolation.

02 · Why

Robust perception outside the laboratory

Real environments contain occlusion, viewpoint changes, unfamiliar layouts, and incomplete evidence. These are central challenges for systems intended to assist people.

03 · How

World models, complementary sensing, and embodiment

I plan to connect visual, WiFi, and mmWave observations through shared spatial representations, then evaluate them through meaningful tasks on embodied systems.

Research objective

Build spatially aware embodied systems whose performance is measured not only by benchmark accuracy, but by reliability, generalisation, and practical value in human-centred environments.

Towards Spatial Supersensing in the Wild

Tianjun Gu*, Tianyu Xin*, Kuan Zhang*, Bowen Yang, Kok-Chung Chua, et al.

Summary. Introduces VSI-Super-Wild, a benchmark for coherent spatial reasoning over long, real-world video. Its 442 videos and 6,980 verified question-answer pairs probe world state across the agent, objects, and environment.

Weitao Xiong, Tianyu Liu, Peng Li, Kok Chung Chua, Toa Chean Khim, Pu Wang, Hongfei Xue

Summary. Combines inverse rendering for direct reflections with 3D Gaussian Splatting for site-specific multipath, enabling realistic radar-signal synthesis from dynamic human meshes.
